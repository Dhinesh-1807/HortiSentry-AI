# HortiSentry Trusted Agricultural Source Policy

## Source Hierarchy
HortiSentry strictly enforces source quality tiers for evidence generation.

| Tier | Institution / Organization | Domain | Authority Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1** | ICAR — Indian Council of Agricultural Research | `icar.gov.in` | 1.00 |
| **Tier 1** | TNAU Agritech Portal | `agritech.tnau.ac.in` | 1.00 |
| **Tier 1** | FAO — Food and Agriculture Organization | `fao.org` | 0.95 |
| **Tier 1** | EPPO — European Plant Protection Org | `gd.eppo.int` | 0.95 |
| **Tier 1** | PPQS — Directorate of Plant Protection, India | `ppqs.gov.in` | 1.00 |
| **Tier 2** | CABI Plantwise Knowledge Bank | `plantwiseplusknowledgebank.org` | 0.85 |
| **Tier 2** | ICAR-IIHR / ICAR-CPRI / ICAR-CISH / ICAR-NRCB | `iihr.res.in` | 0.85 |
| **Tier 3** | State Agricultural Extension Services | `extension.org` | 0.70 |

## Exclusion Criteria
Unverified blog posts, commercial seller marketing pages, and unreferenced forum comments are strictly disallowed from evidence ingestion.
